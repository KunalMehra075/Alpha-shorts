1. **Changed from Curiosity-Driven to Mystery-Driven Storytelling**

   * Instead of "here are some facts", every video revolves around a mystery, question, claim, or investigation.

2. **Added Completion Compulsion**

   * The viewer should feel that leaving early means missing the answer or resolution.

3. **Introduced a Fixed Story Structure**

   * Hook/Mystery → Evidence → Escalation → Twist → Resolution → Discussion Question.

4. **Made Facts Serve the Story**

   * Every fact must help answer the central question instead of being random information.

5. **Added Universal Relatability**

   * Connect topics to humans, daily life, survival, technology, money, future, or human curiosity whenever possible.

6. **Added Absurdity Factor**

   * Include at least one unbelievable-but-true fact that makes viewers think, "How is this even possible?"

7. **Added Open Loops**

   * Continuously tease upcoming information:
   * "लेकिन असली रहस्य अभी बाकी है..."
   * "और यहीं से कहानी और अजीब हो जाती है..."

8. **Added Curiosity Spikes Every 10–15 Seconds**

   * A shocking number, discovery, contradiction, consequence, or visual reveal to reset attention.

9. **Added a Mid-Video Twist**

   * Around the middle, introduce conflicting evidence or a surprising discovery that changes the viewer's assumptions.

10. **Strengthened the Final Reveal**

    * The most interesting fact is saved for the last 5 seconds instead of being revealed early.

11. **Added Shareability Requirement**

    * At least one fact should be something viewers naturally want to tell friends or family.

12. **Improved Topic Selection Rules**

    * Prefer mysteries, discoveries, lost civilizations, unexplained phenomena, future predictions, and surprising science over plain informational topics.

13. **Added Visual Thinking**

    * Every sentence should naturally create a visual scene, helping with fast-paced editing and retention.

14. **Removed Fact-List Syndrome**

    * Prevents scripts from becoming:

      * Fact 1
      * Fact 2
      * Fact 3
      * Fact 4
    * and turns them into a narrative journey.

15. **Added "Wait... What?" Moments**

    * Every script should contain at least one moment that challenges what the viewer currently believes.

16. **Made the Viewer the Main Character**

    * Whenever possible, frame the topic around:

      * What it means for humans
      * What it means for Earth
      * What it means for the future
      * What it means for the viewer

17. **Optimized for Retention Psychology Rather Than Information Density**

    * The original prompt optimized for delivering information.
    * The new prompt optimizes for keeping people watching until the end.
